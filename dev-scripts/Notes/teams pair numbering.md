## Pair Numbering for *TEAMS*


*The first number and the second number are always from opposing teams.*
There are two types of teams movements.

### Type 1:
In each team of four, there is a designated NS pair and a designated EW pair. The NS pair plays NS in every matchup, and the EW pair play EW in every matchup.

For this type, the first number is the team number of the NS pair (and also their pair number). The second number is the team number of the EW pair; if needed, their pair number is their team number plus the total number of teams.

Example: There are 15 teams in the competition. The movement line for table 1 starts 1,11,4, ...

That means that in round 1, the NS pair from team 1 (pair number 1) play against the EW pair from team 11 (who are pair number 26). They play board set 4 against each other.


### Type 2:
Each of the two pairs from each team of four sometimes plays NS during the session, and sometimes plays EW.

For this type, the first number is the pair number of the NS pair. The second number is the pair number of the EW pair. Each team number can if needed be got from each pair number by subtracting the total number of teams.

Example: There are 15 teams in the competition. The movement line for table 1 starts 25,13,4, ...

That means that in round 1, pair number 25 plays against pair number 13, board set 3. Which teams are they from? pair 25 is from team 10 (subtract 15, the total number of teams). Pair 13 is from team 13 (you can't subtract 15 from 13, so you don't need to).
